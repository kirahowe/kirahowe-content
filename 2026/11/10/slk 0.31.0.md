slk 0.31.0 adds more click handling, tab-completion for `:` command names, and a preview of matched commands in the status bar. `:version` is also updated to show the build you are running and the update hint when a newer one is available. `:help` shows every `:` command.

There are also many bug fixes, details are in the release notes.
